About MindWatch 2.0 & Ethical Architecture

MindWatch is an AI-assisted social media language screening and student mental wellbeing platform. Designed to promote early self-reflection, provide evidence-based coping exercises, and lower friction to professional help.

Core Ethical Commitments

  • A screening aid, not a diagnostic tool: Output can be wrong and does NOT constitute a clinical depression or anxiety diagnosis.
  • Raw text is not stored by default: Only the risk result and linguistic aggregates are kept (and linked to you only if you log in).
  • Consent-driven usage: Only analyse your own text, or text you have explicit consent to analyse.
  • Crisis override safety: Explicit crisis language always triggers help-line information, regardless of the model score.
  • Language safety guard: The model reads English only; other scripts are reported as "unsupported", never as "low risk".
  • Data sovereignty: You can export or delete all your data at any time via your account dashboard.

Linguistic Biomarkers

Clinical NLP literature demonstrates that individuals experiencing emotional distress often exhibit:

  • Elevated First-Person Singular Pronouns: Reflecting cognitive self-focus and social isolation (Rude et al., 2004).
  • Absolutist Vocabulary: Words like 'always', 'never', 'completely' indicating all-or-nothing cognitive distortions (Al-Mosaiwi & Johnstone, 2018).
  • Affective Valence Imbalance: Elevated negative affective markers compared to mitigating positive words.

Validated Instruments

MindWatch 2.0 incorporates public-domain, gold-standard psychological screening tools:

  • PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9): Kroenke, Spitzer & Williams (2001). 9 validated items with standard 0–27 scoring.
  • GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7): Spitzer, Kroenke, Williams & Löwe (2006). 7 validated items with standard 0–21 scoring.
  • Non-Diagnostic Student Screeners: Dedicated educational checks for sleep habits, academic workload pressure, and motivation.
