Immediate Calming Exercises

Box Breathing Technique (4-4-4-4):

Inhale for 4s → Hold for 4s → Exhale for 4s → Hold for 4s. Calms the sympathetic fight-or-flight nervous response.

5-4-3-2-1 Sensory Grounding:

Identify 5 things you can see, 4 feel, 3 hear, 2 smell, 1 taste. Anchors racing thoughts back to physical reality.

The 20-Minute Sleep Rule:

If unable to fall asleep after 20 minutes, leave bed and sit in dim light reading something peaceful until sleepy.

When to Seek In-Person Care

  • Low mood, emptiness, or severe sorrow lasting continuously for more than 2 weeks
  • Inability to sleep or eat normally that impairs class attendance or work
  • Intense panic attacks or overwhelming dread that doesn't subside
  • Persistent thoughts of hurting yourself, suicide, or wishing you were dead
  • Severe social withdrawal where you stop communicating with all friends and family
